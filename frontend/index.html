01 Project Mission

Clinical Transparency

Every prediction is accompanied by a saliency-based activation map that highlights the spatial reasoning behind the model's output, enabling clinicians to interrogate and contextualise AI-assisted findings.

Rapid Analysis

OncoStream processes medical images in milliseconds, providing near-instantaneous classification results that can support radiologists during high-volume screening workflows.

Research Integrity

Developed strictly for research and educational contexts. All outputs carry explicit disclaimers reinforcing that AI results must be reviewed and validated by qualified medical professionals.

03 Ethical Notice
For Research and Educational Use Only

OncoStream is an experimental research platform. All outputs, predictions, and confidence scores generated by this system are intended solely for academic investigation and educational demonstration. They do not constitute clinical diagnoses and must not be used as a substitute for professional medical evaluation. Any application of this system in clinical settings without proper regulatory approval and medical oversight is strictly discouraged.
